# E4 — Two statins on cholesterol-homeostasis signalling

## Prompt

> Compare Pitavastatin and Pravastatin at 5 µM on cholesterol-homeostasis signalling: HMGCR, LDLR, INSIG1, HMGCS1 expression and the HALLMARK cholesterol homeostasis gene set, across lines.

## Expected tools

`search_conditions` (labels `Pitavastatin (Calcium)`, `Pravastatin (sodium)`), `resolve_genes`, `get_differential_expression(drug=[both], genes=[...], dose=5)`, `search_gene_sets(query="CHOLESTEROL", collection="HALLMARK")`, `get_pathway_scores(gene_sets=["HALLMARK_CHOLESTEROL_HOMEOSTASIS"], drug=[both], dose=5)`.

## Hard criteria

- Both salt-form labels are found and used verbatim.
- Direction: HMGCR, LDLR, INSIG1 and HMGCS1 **increase** and the gene set rises; pitavastatin shows the larger effect in most lines.
- Pathway scores are used as served (never vehicle-subtracted again) and no p-value is claimed for them.
- Differential-expression `padj` is reported per contrast; a NULL `padj` is called "untested", not "no effect".
- No claim that greater on-target potency is proven.
- Plates are compared like for like (the two drugs share one plate; a second pravastatin plate is reported separately).
