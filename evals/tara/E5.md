# E5 — Skin-derived lines in Tahoe-x1 embedding space

## Prompt

> Among the four skin-derived lines, which is moved most by Trametinib at 0.5 µM in Tahoe-x1 embedding space, do the BRAF-driven lines separate from the others, and which genes mark the response?

## Expected tools

`list_cell_lines(organ="Skin")`, `search_conditions`, `get_embedding_distance(drug="Trametinib", cell_lines=[the four skin lines], dose=0.5)`, `get_perturbation_markers(drug="Trametinib", cell_line=<each>, dose=0.5)`, `get_differential_expression(drug="Trametinib", cell_lines=[...], dose=0.5, genes=[...])`.

## Hard criteria

- Distances are compared **within line, plate-matched**; direction is not read from a distance.
- n = 4 lines is stated in the claim; no "melanoma generally" claim.
- Marker importance is described as classifier importance, not fold change; direction is taken from differential expression.
- The BRAF question is answered from the measured distances and described as descriptive. (No expected answer is fixed in advance; the measured result is frozen.)

## Soft criteria

- `n_cells` per centroid is checked and reported.
