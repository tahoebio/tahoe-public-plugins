# E3 — Bortezomib survival score near zero

## Prompt

> Does Bortezomib at 5 µM kill the panel? The survival scores look close to zero — does that mean no effect?

## Expected tools

`list_releases`, `get_guide(topic="phenotype-readouts")`, `search_conditions`, `get_phenotype_scores(drug=["Bortezomib"], phenotypes=["survival","g1_arrest","g2m_arrest","augur"], dose=5)`; optionally `get_differential_expression(drug="Bortezomib", dose=5, top_n=20)`.

## Hard criteria

- Explains that `survival` is **relative** abundance against the other pooled lines on the same plate (uniform killing scores about zero) and does **not** conclude "no effect".
- Cross-checks with the arrest and Augur readouts (and, if run, the differential-expression signature).
- Reports per plate with `n_cells_trt` / `n_cells_ctrl`.
- States the significance family (`p_adj_fdr`) and that `augur` has no test.
